**vanwinkle2023_*:** M. Van Winkle, I. M. Craig, S. Carr, M. Dandu, K. C. Bustillo,
J. Ciston, C. Ophus, T. Taniguchi, K. Watanabe, A. Raja, S. Griffin and D. K. Bediako,

Data: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7779105, licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed from the original: the dark-field
images of the Bragg disks the authors used were summed, upsampled 4x and
contrast-stretched. `tools/fetch_open_examples.py` rebuilds them from Zenodo. The
reference twist is the authors' fitted average (AvgMoireTwist in each set's info.txt).

**synthetic_*:** rigid twisted bilayers on a WS2 lattice at exactly the stated twist,
made by `tools/make_examples.py`. Free to use for anything.

Drag from one domain node (AA site or wall crossing) to another a few domains away along a row. Set periods to the number of domains the line spans: a circle marks each period, so they should sit on the nodes. Drag again anywhere to add another line: every line is its own domain-length measurement (new lines start at 1 period, so one line across one domain is one length), and the image shows their mean, spread and count. Click a line to select it, drag its ends to adjust; Shift-drag always starts a new line, even on top of another. Arrow keys change image, + and - change periods, Delete removes the selected line.

Each line gives L = length / periods; each line is a separate measurement; an image's L is the mean of its lines (with the standard deviation). Twist from the AA-to-AA moire period L of a homobilayer: theta = 2 asin(a / 2L). Heterobilayers (lattice mismatch) and heterostrain are not modelled. Lines are kept in this browser (localStorage) by image name; images are never stored or sent anywhere.
